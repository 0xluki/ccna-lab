Step 13

Final Verification

Goal. Prove every layer of the lab works, from cables to security.
Concept. Network verification works bottom-up: Layer 1 (cables/ports) → Layer 2 (VLANs, trunks, STP) → Layer 3 (IPs, routing) → services (DHCP) → security (ACL, AAA). If something fails, fix the lowest broken layer first.
Before you start. Steps 1–12 finished.

Your tasks

  1. Layer 2: show vlan brief, show interfaces trunk, show etherchannel summary and show spanning-tree vlan 10 on both switches.
  2. Layer 3: show ip interface brief and show ip route on R1/R2; confirm OSPF neighbour FULL.
  3. DHCP: PC1–PC3 hold leases and ping their gateways.
  4. Connectivity: PC1 and PC3 reach the server, PC2 cannot, PCs reach each other.
  5. Security: PC4 can SSH to R1, R2, SW1, SW2; PC1 cannot SSH to R1; BPDU Guard err-disables a rogue switch port.
  6. Save every device (copy run start) and save the .pkt file.
